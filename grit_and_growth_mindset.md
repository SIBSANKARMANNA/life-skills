# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1 — Grit

* Grit means staying passionate and continuing to work hard toward your long-term goals, even when things are difficult.
* It means continuing to work hard despite difficulties, failures, and setbacks, and treating success as a marathon rather than a sprint.

## Question 2 — Introduction to Growth Mindset

* A growth mindset is the belief that our skills and intelligence can be developed through effort, learning, challenges, mistakes, and feedback.
* Instead of focusing only on results or avoiding failure, we focus on the process of improving and learning over time.

## Question 3 — Understanding Internal Locus of Control

* **Internal locus of control** means believing that our own actions, effort, and decisions influence the results we achieve.
* The key point is to focus on the things that are within our control instead of blaming external circumstances.
* We should take responsibility for our actions and look for what we can change or improve.
* Taking action and seeing the results of our own effort helps build a stronger sense of control and motivation.

## Question 4 — How to Build a Growth Mindset

* Believe in my ability to figure things out.
* Believe that I can learn and get better.
* Question assumptions that limit my abilities.
* Do not let my current knowledge and skills limit my future possibilities.
* Create my own learning curriculum.
* Continuously learn through books, courses, seminars, and other resources.
* Treat difficulties and challenges as part of the learning process.
* Honor the struggle instead of giving up when things become difficult.
* Build resilience when facing failure and setbacks.
* Stay calm during difficult situations and use them as opportunities to learn and grow.

## Question 5 — My Ideas to Take Action and Build Growth Mindset

* Take 100% responsibility for my learning.
* Stay with a problem until I understand and solve it instead of quitting.
* Treat confusion as a signal that I need more understanding.
* Treat errors as information about what is not working and learn how to fix them.
* Put in more effort when a concept is difficult instead of becoming discouraged.
* Learn from documentation, Google, Stack Overflow, GitHub Issues, and other reliable resources before asking for help.
* Never write code that I do not understand.
* Break difficult problems into smaller parts and solve them step by step.
* Treat new concepts and difficult situations as learning opportunities.
* Accept feedback and use it to improve my skills.
