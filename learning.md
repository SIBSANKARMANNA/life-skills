## What is Learning?
Learning is the lifelong process of acquiring knowledge, skills, attitudes, or behaviors through study, experience, observation, or practice. It changes how we think, make decisions, and solve problems. Learning is not limited to formal education—it happens throughout life in personal, academic, and professional settings.


### How brain learns?
The brain learns by creating and strengthening connections between neurons, a process known as neuroplasticity. Repeated practice, feedback, and meaningful experiences reinforce these neural pathways, making knowledge easier to recall and apply. Sleep, attention, and emotion also play important roles in memory formation.

### Stages of learning?
A common model describes learning as progressing through four stages:

Unconscious Incompetence – You don't know what you don't know.
Conscious Incompetence – You recognize your knowledge gaps.
Conscious Competence – You can perform the skill with effort.
Unconscious Competence – The skill becomes automatic through practice.


### Feynman Technique
Developed from the learning approach of physicist Richard Feynman, this technique improves understanding by explaining concepts in simple language. If you struggle to explain a topic clearly, it reveals gaps in your knowledge. Revisiting those gaps and simplifying your explanation leads to deeper learning.


### Bloom's Taxonomy
Bloom's Taxonomy organizes learning into six cognitive levels, from basic recall to creating new ideas. It helps educators design lessons and learners measure their understanding.


### Kolb's Learning Cycle
Kolb proposed that effective learning is a continuous cycle involving experience, reflection, conceptual understanding, and experimentation. Each stage builds on the previous one, helping learners continuously improve.

### Growth Mindset
A growth mindset is the belief that intelligence and abilities can be developed through effort, learning, and persistence. People with this mindset see mistakes as opportunities to improve rather than signs of failure.


### Deliberate Practice
Deliberate practice focuses on improving specific skills through structured practice, immediate feedback, and repeated refinement. It emphasizes quality over quantity and targets areas of weakness.

### Active Recall & Spaced Repetition
Active Recall strengthens memory by retrieving information without looking at notes. Spaced Repetition schedules reviews over increasing intervals, improving long-term retention and reducing forgetting.


### Common Mistakes While Learning
Many learners slow their progress by relying on passive reading, avoiding difficult topics, multitasking, skipping review sessions, or studying without clear goals. Effective learning requires active engagement, regular practice, and consistent reflection.


### Tips for Lifelong Learning
Lifelong learning involves continuously developing knowledge and skills throughout life. Stay curious, read regularly, ask questions, practice consistently, seek feedback, teach others, and reflect on your progress. Small, consistent improvements often lead to significant long-term growth.
