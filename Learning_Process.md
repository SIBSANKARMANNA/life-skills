# Learning Process

## Question 1

### What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where I explain a concept in simple words, identify what I don't understand, and study those weak areas until I can explain the concept clearly.

## Question 3

### What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active Mode:** This is when I give my full attention to a specific problem or concept and think about it carefully using knowledge I already have.
- **Diffuse Mode:** This is a relaxed state of thinking where the mind can make wider connections and come up with new ideas or perspectives.

Both modes are useful for learning. When I get stuck, I can move away from the problem for a while and then return to it with a fresh perspective.

## Question 5

### What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

Going forward, I will try to make my learning more focused and practical.

- I will break difficult concepts into smaller parts instead of trying to learn everything at once.
- I will use the Feynman Technique to explain concepts in my own words and identify areas where I am weak.
- I will use active recall by closing my notes and trying to remember what I have learned instead of repeatedly reading the same material.
- I will practice concepts repeatedly instead of only understanding them theoretically.
- I will use Pomodoro sessions to work with focused attention for a fixed period and then take a short break.
- I will reduce distractions by keeping my phone on silent and avoiding unnecessary notifications while studying.
- I will take proper breaks when I am stuck so that I can use both focus and diffuse modes of thinking.
- I will practice consistently instead of waiting until the last moment.
- I will try to teach concepts to someone else, because if I can explain something clearly, it means I understand it better.
- I will focus on making the learning process enjoyable and approach new topics with curiosity instead of fear of making mistakes.
